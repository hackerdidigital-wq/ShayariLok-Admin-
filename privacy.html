1. Who we are

CyberKing Studio publishes the Shayari Lok Android application (com.cyberking.shayarilok) and official web portal. Our platform delivers curated Hindi and Hinglish Shayari, Photo Shayari cards, and daily Suvichar thoughts without requiring any mandatory user registration or account sign-up.

2. Remote Shayari & Real-Time Configuration

When you open Shayari Lok, the Android app and standalone website fetch the latest Shayari collection, category chips, bell announcements, maintenance notices, and version updates from our read-only public JSON feed. Standard network headers (such as temporary IP delivery by CDN servers) are processed strictly to serve content fast and reliably.

3. Advertising & Third-Party AdMob Services

Advertising is currently enabled in this Android release using Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device Advertising ID and anonymized diagnostic signals to deliver relevant banner or interstitial ads in accordance with Google Privacy Policies.

4. Local Storage & Cookies

We do not set invasive cross-site advertising tracking cookies on our public reader pages. Temporary browser storage may retain your selected category filter or recently loaded Shayari items strictly to keep browsing instant on slower mobile networks.

5. Images, Photo Shayari & External Links

Shayari posts may include visual Photo Shayari cards hosted via CDN, and the website/app may provide external links to our official WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, or Google Play Store pages. External third-party platforms operate under their own respective privacy policies.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal reader information. Only non-personal ad-delivery or crash diagnostic signals handled by Google Play Services / Google AdMob (when ads are enabled) are governed by Google's privacy and security standards.

7. How long we retain your data

Because Shayari Lok does not collect user registration profiles, private messages, comments, or contact lists, we do not maintain personal reader accounts on our servers. Clipboard copies and category choices stay entirely on your own device.

8. What rights you have over your data

You can reset or limit your Android Advertising ID inside device privacy settings, clear local app/browser storage anytime, or contact us directly regarding any content attribution, takedown, or privacy question.

9. Contact, Official Website & Policy URL

For privacy inquiries, copyright concerns, or support requests, email support@shayarilok.in.
